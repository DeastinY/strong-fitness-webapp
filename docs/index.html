Features

Pure signal, no noise.

No accounts, no routines, no meal plans. Just your data, visualized.

📈

Volume trends

Weekly and monthly load charts across all muscle groups. See exactly when you peaked — and when you backed off.

🏆

Personal records

Best weight, estimated 1RM (Epley formula), and best reps per exercise. Sortable table, updated automatically on every import.

📅

Consistency heatmap

12-week calendar heatmap and weekly frequency chart — because showing up is half the battle.

🏃

Cardio tracking

Distance and duration per session, plotted over time alongside your strength volume.

🔍

Per-exercise detail

Full session history per exercise: best weight line, volume bars, and every set logged.

🤖

AI export

One click generates a compact 3-month training log — paste it into any LLM for programming feedback.

Hand your trainer a real report

Generate a PDF-ready progress report for any date range and print it straight from the browser — no export tools needed.

  • Summary: sessions, total volume, avg duration, frequency
  • Attendance calendar with monthly breakdown
  • All-time load chart with selected period highlighted
  • Per-exercise charts + set-by-set table with weight deltas
  • Ctrl + P → PDF, done
